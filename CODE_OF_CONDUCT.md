# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation to be harassment-free and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Focus feedback on code, behavior, and evidence.
- Protect private, customer, payment, and credential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Posting credentials, customer data, payment data, or malicious content.
- Disrupting discussion or repeatedly ignoring project boundaries.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces, issue and PR discussions, and related community channels.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles.
